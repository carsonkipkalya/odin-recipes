Githeri

Description

Githeri is a traditional Kenyan dish made from boiled maize and beans, often enriched with potatoes, onions, tomatoes, and seasonings.

It is a hearty, flavourful meal that provides energy, protein, and fibre, making it a staple in many Kenyan homes.

Ingredients

4 Servings

  • 2 cups boiled maize
  • 2 cups boiled beans
  • 2 medium potatoes
  • 2 medium tomatoes
  • 1 medium onion
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1-2 cups water or stock
  • Salt to taste

Optional: Black pepper or curry powder

Steps

Time: 45-60 minutes (pre-boiled maize and beans) or 2-3 hours (dried maize and beans)

  1. Peel and dice the potatoes, and chop the onions and tomatoes.
  2. Heat cooking oil in a pot and sauté the onions until soft.
  3. Cook the tomatoes until they form a thick sauce.
  4. Stir in the potatoes and cook briefly.
  5. Add the boiled maize and beans, along with water or stock.
  6. Cook until the potatoes are tender and the flavours combine.
  7. Add salt and optional spices, then serve hot.

Tip: For extra flavour, serve githeri with avocado or fresh coriander.
